About DNC Chits

With over three decades of experience, DNC Chits has been a trusted name in financial services, helping thousands of customers achieve their financial goals through secure chit fund schemes.

Our Story

Established in 1990, DNC Chits began with a simple mission: to provide reliable and accessible financial solutions to individuals and businesses. What started as a small local operation has grown into a respected financial institution serving thousands of customers across the region.

Our journey has been guided by principles of transparency, integrity, and customer-centric service. We believe in building long-term relationships with our clients, understanding their unique financial needs, and providing tailored solutions that help them achieve their goals.

Over the years, we've adapted to changing economic landscapes while maintaining our core values. Today, we combine traditional financial wisdom with modern practices to offer secure and rewarding chit fund schemes.
